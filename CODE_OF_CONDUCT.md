# Code of Conduct

Habit Workspace aims to be a respectful, constructive space for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give and receive actionable feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's personal information or
  vault contents.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its documented scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
unwelcome sexual attention, and sharing private information without consent are
not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's private contact channel once configured. Do not report sensitive
details in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports, protect
confidentiality where practical, and may edit or remove content, request a
change in behavior, or restrict participation. Maintainers are expected to
apply these standards consistently.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing
the project in public.
